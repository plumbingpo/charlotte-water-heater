Charlotte Water Heater specializes in food-service hot water, including high-recovery gas tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters for high-temperature dish machines, correct sizing based on peak-hour demand, and scheduled service built around your hours.

Safe Installation

Code-Compliant Installation and Documentation

Badly installed or neglected hot water equipment can create carbon monoxide hazards, scalding, bacterial growth in lukewarm storage, and costly water damage from a ruptured tank. Those are real risks for a commercial property owner responsible for employees, customers, and tenants. Every installation and inspection we perform covers venting, combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge, expansion tanks, mixing valves, and code items like drain pans and seismic straps where required, and each visit ends with a written report listing risks, priorities, and recommended preventive steps.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and health or building inspectors. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature records, relief valve test results, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms.

Emergency Service

Fast Commercial Water Heater Service

When a commercial water heater fails, the clock starts immediately. Dish rooms stall, salon chairs empty, guests call the front desk, and tenants start complaining. Our commercial line gets a technician rolling fast for emergency repairs anywhere in Charlotte and the surrounding area, and planned work can be done after hours to limit downtime.

Failed gas valves, burned-out elements, ignition faults, tripped high-limit switches, leaking tanks, stalled recirculation pumps, tankless lockouts: our technicians handle all of it on commercial equipment. The priority is diagnosing the fault and getting hot water back as quickly as possible, and in a multi-heater system we isolate the failed unit so the others carry the load. Once it's running, we set up a maintenance schedule so it doesn't happen again, and each response is fully documented for your records.

Maintenance Programs

Scheduled Maintenance for Commercial Hot Water

Our commercial maintenance program is built around your property's specific equipment, how the building uses hot water, and your budget. Visits include tank flushing and anode checks, tankless descaling, relief valve testing, burner, flue, and venting inspection, recirculation pump and mixing valve service, and temperature verification at the tap. Mineral-heavy water shortens the life of commercial equipment quickly, so high-use properties usually need more frequent visits.

Next to an emergency water heater replacement, scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction as much, and it sharply cuts the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Properties that stay on a schedule usually see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower energy bills. Give us a call to talk through a plan for your property and budget.
